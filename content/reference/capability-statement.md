## Positioning

> We are not a gym reimbursement perk. We are not another wellness app thrown at
> employees who never log in. We are an accountability-driven performance program

> platform — and we measure engagement, not just access.

> engagement-based reporting your leadership team can act on."

## The business problem (as framed in collateral)

- The American Institute of Stress estimates workplace stress costs U.S.
  employers over **$300 billion annually** in absenteeism, diminished
  productivity, and turnover.
- Burned-out employees are **63% more likely to take sick days** and **23% more
  likely to visit the ER**.
- The average corporate wellness program sees **6–8% employee participation**.

Root cause as stated: generic programs don't create accountability, don't build
habits, and don't give HR leaders evidence anything is working.

## The three tracks

**Track 1 — Live facilitated workshops.** Mike Logan leads 60-minute sessions,
on-site or virtual, built on physical performance, mental resilience, and
sustainable habit architecture. Signature workshops:

- **Battle-Tested Resilience** — stress management and high-pressure execution
  drawn from USMC combat leadership.
- **The Executive Athlete** — precision nutrition and time-efficient movement for
  professionals with no calendar room.
- **Mindfulness in the Modern Workplace** — breathwork, desk-side resets, and
  mental performance tools deployable in under 3 minutes.

**Track 2 — The Now Time digital wellness platform.** White-labeled,
enterprise-grade: personalized programming, a 21-day progressive mindfulness
track, nutrition and habit tracking, progress and community features,
manager-visible engagement dashboards, mobile-first.

**Track 3 — Engagement-based reporting.** The stated differentiator. Each program
period ends with a Corporate Engagement Report: participation by department and
cohort, tool utilization breakdown, completion funnel data, anonymized sentiment
and participation trends. *"You get evidence. Not anecdotes. Evidence."*

## Ideal client profile

50–200 employees · HR / C-suite decision maker · DC/MD/VA primary market · little
or no measurable ROI on current wellness spend.

Also targeted: government contractors, federal agencies, health systems,
universities, and associations in the DMV; organizations with veteran hiring or
supplier diversity programs.

## Program options

- **Option B — Quarterly Performance Program** (recommended starting point): one
  signature workshop + 90-day platform access for the full team + post-program
  engagement report. Flat quarterly fee based on headcount.
- **Option A — Enterprise Wellness Partnership**: recurring workshops (monthly or
  bi-monthly), full-year platform access, ongoing reporting, dedicated account
  management. Per-employee/month, volume pricing available.

Stated sales motion: always start with Option B, use the quarter's data to build
the case for Option A.
