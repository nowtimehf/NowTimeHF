## Three offers are documented, at three price points

| Offer | What's included | Investment |
|---|---|---|
| **The Pilot Sprint** | 60-min live workshop (choice of three) + 30-day platform access for attendees + engagement report | **$1,500–$2,500 one-time**, no long-term commitment |
| **Quarterly Performance Program** ("Option B") | One signature workshop + 90-day platform access for the full team + post-program engagement report | Flat quarterly fee by headcount |
| **Enterprise Wellness Partnership** ("Option A") | Recurring workshops, full-year platform access, ongoing reporting, dedicated account management | Per-employee/month, volume pricing |

The three workshops are constant across all offers: **Battle-Tested Resilience**,
**The Executive Athlete**, **Mindfulness at Work** (also written as "Mindfulness
in the Modern Workplace").

### The Pilot Sprint pitch, verbatim

> Burned-out teams don't need another perk. They need a program that works in
> under 2 minutes a day.

> Most wellness vendors sell a subscription. None sell a result.

> Proof before you scale — no company-wide contract required to start.

Three-step framing: **Book the Call** (15-min discovery) → **Run the Sprint**
(workshop + platform opens same week) → **Review the Data** (30-day engagement
report, then decide on scaling).

## Cost-of-inaction cold openers

Three variants exist for outreach DMs/emails, all built on the same move: ask
what the current perk is actually doing, then name the invisible cost. Pattern:

1. Question about current wellness spend's measurable effect
2. "Most 50–200 person teams I talk to…" — normalize the problem
3. Identify as USMC veteran + founder
4. Soft ask for 10–15 minutes

> Hi [Name], quick question — what is [Company]'s current wellness perk actually
> doing for your sick-day and turnover numbers?

> Hi [Name], can I ask you something — when's the last time anyone actually
> checked whether your team's wellness benefit is getting used?

### ⚠️ Two figures in these openers need checking before reuse

- **"losing $1.75+ per employee per month in avoidable healthcare and
  absenteeism costs"** — this is almost certainly wrong. $1.75/employee/month is
  $21/employee/year, which is trivially small and undercuts the argument it's
  making. It contradicts the capability statement's own framing ($300B across
  the workforce ≈ $1,800/employee/year). Likely a lost order of magnitude or a
  mis-transcribed unit. **Do not reuse until corrected.**
- **"a real coach in every employee's pocket for about $50/month"** — a
  per-employee price point that appears nowhere else in the material and doesn't
  reconcile with the Pilot Sprint's $1,500–$2,500 flat fee. Confirm before
  quoting a price publicly.

A brand whose whole differentiator is "we prove it with data" cannot afford a
loose number in the opener. Both need a decision.
