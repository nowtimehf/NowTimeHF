# Brand Foundations

Source: the Obsidian Marketing IO brand questionnaire (Aug 2024), answered by
Mike Logan directly. This is the earliest first-person account of what the brand
*is* — it outranks anything reconstructed later.

## The name means something

> **NOW = No Other Way. TIME = Time Is My Everything.**
>
> Now Time originally was a workout program I created… The concept is that we do
> not have time to waste and we should use our time wisely. A place where you can
> save time and accomplish your goals the most efficient way, in one place.

This has never appeared in any social post reviewed so far. It's the single
strongest untold story the brand owns — an origin, a founder conviction, and a
name reveal in one. **Use it.**

## Mission, values, USP

- **Mission:** "To bring health and wellness to the masses. From the physical
  fitness to the mental health to the inner being."
- **Core values:** "Training the body and strengthening the mind. Building a
  community that will support one another."
- **Tagline in the founder's own words:** *"We are in this together! Together,
  we are strong!"*
- **USP:** the app is customizable per individual *and* per organization.
- **Long-term goal:** host destination events in other countries.

## Brand personality (founder's answer)

> A visionary — someone always looking for growth and expansion. Someone that
> doesn't settle for less but is patient and vigilant in their goals.

Desired customer feeling: *"well taken care of… like part of a family."*
Experience: *"personal, down to earth. Regular communication."*

## Competitors — corrected

**Nuvita** and **Goomi**, per the founder. Differentiation in his words:

> Nuvita is more of a brand for coaches to use. I am personal and run the
> business. Goomi does not have an app.

> ⚠️ An earlier version of `brand/brief.md` listed **Limeade** as the
> competitor/inspiration. That was introduced during a Q&A and is **not** from
> any source document. Corrected here and in the brief.

## Audience definition — note the discrepancy

| Source | Company size | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Brand questionnaire (2024) | **50–500** employees | hybrid/remote-first, USA-based |
| Capability statement (2026) | **50–200** employees | DC/MD/VA metro, SDVOSB pathways |

The narrower, later definition is the working one — but the **hybrid/remote**
qualifier from 2024 is worth keeping. It's a real targeting filter the later
documents dropped, and it's the exact population a mobile-first program serves
best.

## Platform note

The app is white-labeled on **Everfit** ("regular updates by our amazing
developers from Everfit brand"). Relevant when describing technical capability —
don't imply in-house development.
