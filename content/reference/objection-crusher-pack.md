# Objection-Crusher Content Pack

Source: `objection-crusher-content-pack.md` (Drive). Six LinkedIn posts, one per
sales objection, with matching graphics (`01-classpass` … `06-free-trials`).

**This is a third voice** — distinct from both the reflective social posts in
`past-posts.md` and the collateral voice in `capability-statement.md`. See the
voice map at the bottom of this file.

## The formula (stated explicitly in the source)

> NAME the objection out loud (builds trust) → REFRAME it (shifts the frame) →
> MATH it (HR directors buy numbers) → CTA (one ask).

Stated cadence: **2 posts/week**, rotating. Each post is designed to work three
ways — LinkedIn text post, 60-second talking-head video, or nurture email.

## The six objections

| # | Objection | The reframe |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | "There's ClassPass. Why hire you?" | Access isn't the problem. Accountability is. |
| 02 | "Why not just get everyone a gym membership?" | You don't have a fitness benefit problem. You have a *usage* problem. |
| 03 | "What's the usage rate? How do I know the ROI?" | I don't sell engagement promises. I sell engagement reporting. |
| 04 | "How does what you do actually mitigate burnout?" | Most corporate wellness doesn't touch burnout. It decorates it. |
| 05 | "How do you define success? Weight loss? Blood pressure?" | Success = sustained engagement producing measurable improvement, reported monthly. |
| 06 | "Other apps have free trials. Why you?" | A free app is a tool. I'm a contractor. |

## Reusable lines

These carry the voice better than any description of it:

- "Perks get ignored. Programs get results."
- "Gym membership utilization is the dirtiest secret in corporate wellness."
- "Nobody hiding bad numbers offers to report them monthly."
- "Vague goals protect vendors. Specific goals protect you. I only work with specific."
- "You don't hire a contractor because hammers are expensive — you hire one because you want the thing built."
- "Free trials are free for a reason — they're betting you'll forget to cancel, not betting you'll change."
- "Small yes first. Earn the big yes."
- "I don't promise your people will never be stressed. I build the infrastructure so stress doesn't compound into burnout."
- "Pilot workshop slots for next month are limited by my calendar, not fake scarcity."

## Structural notes worth copying

- **First person, always.** "What I sell," "my model," "I personally intervene."
  The founder is the product — leans hard on USMC service and 15 years coaching.
- **Concedes ground before winning it.** "Fair question." "Best objection I get."
  "Weight loss? Sometimes." Credibility comes from what it declines to claim —
  note post 5 explicitly refusing medical claims: *"That's between your people
  and their doctors — I'm not a medical provider and I won't pretend to be."*
- **CTA is a keyword DM**, not a link: DM "AUDIT" / "TIME" / "DATA" /
  "RESILIENCE" / "METRICS" / "PILOT". Per the deployment notes, every such
  comment goes into the tracker as a hot lead same day.
- **Uses "perk" constantly** — as the category being attacked, never for
  NowTime's own offer. This is consistent with `brand/brief.md`'s rule, which
  bans the word only for describing our own product.

## Voice map — three registers now documented

| | Reflective social (`past-posts.md`) | Objection-crusher (this file) | Collateral (`capability-statement.md`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | build audience, humanize Coach Mike | convert warm prospects | close a scoped deal |

| Stats | none | some, argumentative | heavy, cited |

| Length | 150–250 words | 200–300 words | multi-page |
| Cadence | ~2x/week | 2x/week | n/a |
