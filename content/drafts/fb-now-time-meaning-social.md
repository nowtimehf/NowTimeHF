People ask what the name means. Here it is.

NOW: No Other Way.
TIME: Time Is My Everything.

It started as a workout program I wrote years before any of this was a company. The whole idea was that there's no time to waste — so use what you've got, and use it well.

I wasn't trying to build a brand. I was trying to say something true about how change actually works: not when conditions are perfect, but now, with whatever the day gave you.

That's still the whole thing.

No other way. Time is my everything.

If you've been waiting for a better week to start — this is your sign that it isn't coming. 💪
