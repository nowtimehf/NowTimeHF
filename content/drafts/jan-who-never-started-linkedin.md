January's almost over. If you ran a challenge, the results are in.

Don't look at the leaderboard first.

Look at three lists:

Who never started.
Who started and stopped by week two.
Who's still going.

The third list will be the people who already ran before January. They're great. They weren't your problem.

The first two lists are your program. Those are the people a wellness budget is supposed to reach, and the ones a leaderboard hides.

Send one message to someone on the second list. Not a reminder. A question: "What got in the way?"

That answer is worth more than every number on the board.

What's the most common reason people on your team stopped?
