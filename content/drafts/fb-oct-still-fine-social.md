"I'm fine" is the most common thing people say when they're not.

It isn't dishonesty. It's efficiency. Explaining takes energy you don't have, and the person asking usually doesn't have time for the real answer anyway.

But there's a cost to that shortcut. Say it enough and you start believing it — right up until something makes it impossible to keep saying.

You don't have to tell everyone. You do have to tell someone, occasionally, accurately.

That's not weakness or oversharing. It's maintenance.

When's the last time you gave someone the real answer?
