Try this before your next stressful meeting:

4 seconds in through your nose.
Hold for 4.
4 seconds out.
Repeat 4 times.

That's it. Box breathing — the same tool we used in the Marines to stay sharp under pressure.

60 seconds. No app, no equipment. Works in a parking lot, a hallway, or on mute in a call nobody needs you on.

It doesn't remove the stress. It puts you back in charge of how you meet it, which is usually the part that actually matters.
