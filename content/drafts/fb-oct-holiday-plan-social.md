Make your December plan now, while things are still calm enough to think.

Not a resolution. A floor.

What's the minimum you'll hold through the holidays? Two sessions a week? A daily walk? Sleep before midnight? Pick something small enough that it survives travel, family, and a week of eating badly — because all three are coming.

The people who don't fall off in December aren't more disciplined. They decided in October what "good enough" would look like, so they never had to negotiate with themselves in the moment.

Decide now. Your December self will be too busy to decide well.

What's your December floor? Write it down somewhere you'll see it.
