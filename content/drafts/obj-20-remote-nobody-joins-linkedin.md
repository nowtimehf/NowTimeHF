"Half our team is remote. Nobody joins anything."

I believe it. Look at what they're being asked to join.

A lunch-and-learn at noon Eastern, when the West Coast is in its morning standup.
A step challenge built around office walks.
A "wellness room" in a building they visit twice a month.

That's not a remote engagement problem. It's a program built for the office and mailed to everyone else.

Three rules for hybrid teams:

It lives on their phone, not in the building.
It takes 10 minutes, not an hour blocked on the calendar.
It runs at two times, so every time zone gets a real option.

Then measure participation by location. If remote numbers are half of in-office, the design is the problem, not the people.

What's the last wellness event your remote people could actually attend?
