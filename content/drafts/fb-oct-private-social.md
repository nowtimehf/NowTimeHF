Your health information is yours. Full stop.

I say this because a lot of wellness culture has quietly normalized the opposite — apps that want everything, challenges that put your step count on a leaderboard, programs where "engagement" means visibility.

Some people love that. Plenty don't, and the ones who don't tend to just quietly opt out rather than explain why.

You can take your health seriously and share none of it. Progress doesn't require an audience, and privacy isn't a lack of commitment.

The only person who needs your numbers is you — and whoever you actively choose to let in.

No question today. Just a reminder that opting out of the leaderboard isn't quitting.
