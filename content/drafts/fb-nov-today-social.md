You don't have to wait until Monday.

After a holiday, most people write off the rest of the week and plan a big restart for Monday, or worse, for January.

Here's the thing: there's nothing to restart. One big meal didn't undo anything. Two days off didn't erase three months of work.

Your next meal is a fresh start. Your next walk is a fresh start. Today is a perfectly good day to do the normal thing again.

No punishment workout. No "getting back on track." Just the next ordinary rep.

What's the one normal thing you're doing today? 👇
