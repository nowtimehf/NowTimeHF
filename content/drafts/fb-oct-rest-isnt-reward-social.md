Rest isn't something you earn after the work is done.

That framing is why so many people are exhausted. If rest is the prize for finishing, and the work never finishes, then rest never comes — and the whole thing runs on a debt that keeps compounding.

Sleep isn't a reward. Neither is a day off, a real lunch, or an evening with your phone in another room.

They're the conditions that make the work possible. Same as fuel in the car.

You don't earn the fuel by driving further.

What are you currently treating as a reward that's actually a requirement?
