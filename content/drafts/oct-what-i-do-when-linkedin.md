Every wellness vendor promises engagement. Almost none of them will tell you what happens when it drops.

Here's mine, specifically.

Week one, I get a baseline: self-reported energy, stress, and activity. Not to diagnose anyone — to have a before.

Then monthly, you get real numbers. Logins, sessions completed, mindfulness minutes, habit streaks, by department and cohort. Good months and bad ones. I don't send the good ones and go quiet on the rest.

When participation drops in a cohort — and it will, usually around week three when novelty wears off — that's my problem to fix that week. I look at where people stopped, I reach out to the ones who went dark, and I change the programming if the programming is the reason.

That's the actual product. Not the app. Not the workshop. The fact that someone whose name is on the business is watching the number and answering for it.

You're not buying software. You're buying a person who notices.

Any vendor can promise engagement. Ask what happens in month three when it dips — and whether they'll show you the number that says it did.
