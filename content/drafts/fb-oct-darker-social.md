The clocks change this month, and most people's routines quietly fall apart with them.

It's predictable. Dark at 5pm, the after-work walk stops. Dark in the morning, the early workout stops. Nothing dramatic — the habit just loses the conditions it was built on.

Then it gets blamed on discipline in January.

Here's the fix, and it has to happen before the change, not after: move the thing you care about most into daylight now. Lunch, mid-afternoon, whenever your daylight actually is.

Habits don't usually die from lack of willpower. They die because the conditions changed and nobody adjusted the plan.

What's the one thing you want to still be doing in December? Move it to daylight this week.
