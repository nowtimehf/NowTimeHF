Working from home is quietly hard on the body.

Nobody warns you about this part. You gained back a commute — and lost the only two walks that were guaranteed in your day.

Meetings run back to back because there's no room to walk between them. Lunch happens at the desk, or doesn't happen. The workday has no edges, so it leaks into the evening.

None of that feels dramatic. It just accumulates.

Three things that fix most of it:

▸ A fake commute. Ten minutes outside before you start, ten when you finish. It's not about steps — it's about a boundary.
▸ Stand up between calls. Every call. Even for 60 seconds.
▸ Eat somewhere that isn't your desk.

Small. Boring. Genuinely effective.

Which one are you worst at? I'll go first: the fake commute, in winter.
