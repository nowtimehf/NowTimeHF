Marine Corps. Then the gym floor. Now this.

I deployed with the Marines during Operation Iraqi Freedom. Came home, and spent the next 15 years in gyms — personal training, boxing, managing floors, writing programs.

Different worlds. Same lesson, over and over:

People don't change because they got inspired. They change because someone built them a structure and then actually checked whether they were doing it.

That's it. That's the whole method. Everything else is decoration.

I've never seen it fail, and I've never seen anything else reliably work.

Whatever you're trying to change right now — who's checking in on you? If the answer is nobody, that's the gap. Not your willpower.
