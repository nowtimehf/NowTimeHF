It probably wasn't willpower.

Most people who quit a fitness plan didn't run out of discipline. They ran out of a plan that fit their life.

Six days a week when you have four. Meal prep that assumes a free Sunday. A gym across town in January. An app that pings you at 6am when your kid is up at 5.

You didn't fail that plan. You outlasted a bad design.

The version that works is almost always smaller than the version you're ashamed of not doing. Three days you'll actually hit beats six you'll abandon by week three.

Start with what survives a bad week. Build from there.

What's realistically achievable for you in a normal week? Start there instead.
