Leadership doesn't approve wellness budgets. They approve reports.

Here's the one-page format I'd bring to any budget meeting. Save it.

1. Invited: how many people had access.
2. Started: how many used it at least once.
3. Active at week four: how many were still using it a month in.
4. Drop-off point: the week most people stopped.
5. What we changed because of #4.

That's it. Five lines.

Most wellness reports lead with satisfaction scores and a testimonial. A CFO skips both. They want to know if the money reached people, and what you did when it didn't.

Line 5 is the one that wins the room. It shows someone is running the program, not just paying for it.

If you showed your leadership these five lines today, which one would be hardest to fill in?
