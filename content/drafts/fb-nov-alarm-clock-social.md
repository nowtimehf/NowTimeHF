Motivation is a terrible alarm clock.

It shows up when it feels like it. Some mornings it's there, most mornings it isn't, and it never once called ahead to warn you.

A calendar doesn't care how you feel. That's the whole point.

Put the workout in your calendar like a meeting with someone you respect. Same time, same days. When it comes up, you don't decide whether to go. You just go, because the decision was already made on Sunday.

The people who look disciplined usually aren't fighting themselves every day. They just stopped asking.
