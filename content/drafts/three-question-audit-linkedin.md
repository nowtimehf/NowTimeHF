The 3-question audit I run before any company works with us:

1. What's your wellness benefit's actual utilization rate?
Not enrollment. Usage. Most leaders have never seen this number, and the vendor rarely volunteers it.

2. When did you last ask employees what support they'd actually use?
A survey from two years ago doesn't count. Neither does a guess from leadership about what people want.

3. Who follows up when someone disengages?
If the answer is "no one," you've found the design flaw. Everything else is downstream of this.

Companies that can answer all three rarely need me. They already have the two things that make wellness work: visibility and ownership.

Companies that can't are usually spending real money on a program that's quietly doing nothing — while turnover does the damage at 6–9 months of salary per departure. (SHRM)

Run it on your own program this week. You don't need a vendor to do it, and the answers will tell you more than any proposal I could send you.
