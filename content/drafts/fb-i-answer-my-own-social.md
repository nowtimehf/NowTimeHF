When you message this page, you get me. Not a team, not a chatbot, not an assistant working from a script.

That's a deliberate choice, and it does limit how many people I can work with at once.

I've made peace with that. Fifteen years of coaching taught me that the thing that changes outcomes isn't the program document — it's whether someone actually notices when you disappear for two weeks.

You can't outsource noticing. The moment I hand that off, I'm selling the same thing as everyone else.

So: smaller roster, real attention.

If you've been circling something and want a straight answer about it, just ask. That's what this is for.
