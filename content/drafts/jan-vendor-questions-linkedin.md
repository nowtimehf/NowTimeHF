5 questions to ask any wellness vendor before you sign. Save this for your next renewal.

1. What's your participation rate at month three, not month one?
2. Can I see a real (anonymized) engagement report?
3. Who on your side notices when my people stop showing up?
4. What do you do about it when they do?
5. What happens if we don't hit the number we agree on?

The industry average for wellness participation is 6–8%. Any vendor can sell access. These questions show you who can sell usage.

Watch for vague answers. "Engagement is strong across our client base" is not a number.

I'd ask me the same five. That's the standard I report against.
