Most of the wellness industry runs on New Year's resolutions. That's the problem.

January is the best month of the year for sign-ups. Gyms know it. Apps know it. Wellness vendors know it.

And almost everyone knows what happens next. The motivation that fills the gym in the first week of January is mostly gone by February. Not because people are lazy. Because motivation is a terrible system. It shows up when it wants to and leaves when things get busy.

A program built on January energy will look great in a January report. Then it will slide toward the industry average: 6–8% participation.

What works instead is boring:

Start smaller than people expect. Ten minutes, not an hour.
Make it social and scheduled, so showing up doesn't depend on how someone feels that day.
Check in when the drop happens, around week two or three, instead of sending another motivational email in week six.

Fifteen years of coaching individuals taught me this before I ever worked with a company: people don't change when they're inspired. They change when someone builds them a structure and then actually checks in.

Your 2027 program doesn't need more January hype. It needs a February plan.

What's your plan for the week participation drops?
