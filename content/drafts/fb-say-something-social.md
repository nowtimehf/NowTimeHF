Most people who are struggling never say it out loud.

Not because they're hiding it. Because saying it feels like admitting something — that you can't handle what everyone else seems to be handling fine.

So it stays internal. And internal is where it gets heavier.

Here's what 15 years of coaching taught me: almost nobody is handling it as well as they look. The person you're comparing yourself to is comparing themselves to someone else.

Telling one person changes the weight of it. That's not weakness, that's just how it works.

Coach, friend, partner, anyone. Just not nobody.

You can drop it here too, if here is easier. 💬
