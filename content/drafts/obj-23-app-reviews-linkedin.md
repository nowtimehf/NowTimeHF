"Our wellness app has great reviews."

I believe you. Plenty of them are well built.

But a 4.8-star rating measures the people who use the app. It says nothing about the people on your team who never opened it.

Reviews measure the app. Usage measures your team.

Ask your vendor for three numbers:

How many of your people logged in last month.
How many logged in this month.
How many logged in only once.

The third number is the one nobody volunteers. It's also where most of the money goes.

The average wellness program reaches 6–8% of employees. A great app with no one checking usage still lands there.

Do you know your team's login numbers off the top of your head?
