Most people wait until something breaks before they get help.

The back goes out. The bloodwork comes back off. The doctor uses a word that gets your attention.

And then, suddenly, there's time. Suddenly the schedule bends.

I'm not judging that — I've done it myself. But it's worth noticing that we treat our health like an emergency service instead of maintenance.

You don't need a crisis to justify taking care of yourself. Feeling steadily better is reason enough.

The best time to build the habit is while everything still seems fine. That's also the hardest time to be convinced of it.

What's one thing you've been putting off until it becomes a problem?
