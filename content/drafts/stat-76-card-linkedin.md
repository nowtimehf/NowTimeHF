76% of employees hit burnout at work at least sometimes. (Gallup)

The average corporate wellness program reaches about 8% of them.

That gap is the entire argument, and it's worth sitting with before you renew anything this month.

Because the 8% isn't random. Voluntary, unstructured programs recruit the people who were already going to be fine — the ones with the schedule, the habit, and the motivation already in place. They're great employees. They weren't the problem.

The 76% includes your quiet high performers. The ones still delivering. The ones nobody's worried about.

You are, right now, paying for a benefit that systematically misses the population it was bought to help. Not because the vendor is dishonest — because voluntary access self-selects, and nobody designed against that.

The fix is structural, not motivational: start with a live session everyone attends, then follow up with whoever goes quiet.

If you only check one number during enrollment this year, make it utilization. Not enrollment. Usage.
