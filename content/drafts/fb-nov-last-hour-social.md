Tomorrow starts the hour before you go to sleep.

Most people judge their day by the morning. But the morning is mostly decided the night before — by how late you scrolled, what time the lights went off, and whether your brain ever got the signal that the day was over.

One change that helps more than you'd expect: phone charges outside the bedroom.

Not on the nightstand. Not face-down. In another room.

You'll hate it for three nights. Then you'll wonder why you didn't do it years ago.

Who's trying it this week? 🙋
