Here's exactly what the Pilot Sprint is, because "pilot" can mean anything.

One 60-minute live workshop. Your team picks one of three: Battle-Tested Resilience, The Executive Athlete, or Mindfulness at Work. I run it live, and attendance is the point — it's the one moment everyone is in the same room at the same time.

Thirty days of platform access for everyone who attended. Short programming built to fit a lunch break, not a 90-minute gym session.

One engagement report at the end. Who used it, how often, where people dropped off, and self-reported energy and stress against their own baseline. Aggregate only. No individual health data, because I never collect it.

$1,500–$2,500 depending on headcount. One time. No contract after it.

That's the whole thing.

It exists because the worst version of this industry asks you to commit a year before you've seen it work on your actual people. I'd rather earn the second conversation with a number than argue for it with a brochure.

If the report is good, you walk into your next budget meeting holding evidence. If it isn't, you've learned something real about your team for the cost of a single workshop.
