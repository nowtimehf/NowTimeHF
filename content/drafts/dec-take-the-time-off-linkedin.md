No pitch this week. One request for every leader reading this.

Take the time off. And let your team see you do it.

Your people don't learn what's acceptable from the policy. They learn it from you. If you send emails on December 26th, the message is that December 26th is a workday, whatever the handbook says.

So this week:

Set the out-of-office and mean it.
Don't schedule-send emails to land while everyone is off. They still read as "my boss was working."
If something truly can't wait, call the one person who needs to know. Don't copy the whole team.

Rest isn't a reward for finishing the year. It's what makes the next one possible. The Marine Corps treats leave as part of readiness, not a break from it. That lesson transfers to every team I've worked with since.

The teams that come back sharp in January are usually the ones whose leaders actually disappeared for a few days.

See you on the other side.
