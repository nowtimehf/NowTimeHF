Travelling for the holidays? You don't need a gym. You need a floor and 20 minutes.

Here's the whole hotel-room workout:

▸ 10 squats
▸ 10 push-ups (knees are fine)
▸ 10 lunges each leg
▸ 30-second plank

Repeat until 20 minutes is up. No equipment, no excuses, no driving anywhere.

The trip isn't the problem. Deciding before you pack is the fix.

Save this for your next trip. 🔖
