76% of employees experience burnout at work at least sometimes. (Gallup)

Read that as a headcount, not a percentage.

On a 10-person team, that's 7 or 8 people.
At 120 employees, it's about 90.

That's not a small group who need extra support. That's most of the room in your next all-hands.

Now look at your wellness program. The industry average reaches 6–8% of employees.

So the question isn't "do we have a burnout problem?"

It's "why is our answer built for 8 people when the problem is 90?"

If you had to guess, how many people on your team used your wellness benefit last month?
