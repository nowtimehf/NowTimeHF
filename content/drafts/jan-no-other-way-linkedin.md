New year, same name on the door. Here's what it means.

Most people assume "Now Time" is a slogan.

It's two acronyms.

NOW: No Other Way.
TIME: Time Is My Everything.

It started as a workout program I created. The idea was simple: we don't have time to waste, so use it on what actually works.

That's still the whole philosophy, now applied to companies.

Not another app nobody opens. Not a January kickoff that's forgotten by February. A set time, a small habit, and someone who notices when you're missing.

Resolutions depend on how you feel in February. A standard doesn't.

What's the one standard your team actually runs on?
