A short week is still a week.

Thanksgiving week is where a lot of routines quietly end. Not on purpose. The schedule changes, there's travel, there's family, and "I'll pick it back up Monday" turns into January.

You don't need your full routine this week. You need your floor.

Two short sessions. A walk on Thursday. Bed at a decent hour once or twice. That's enough to keep the thread, and keeping the thread is the whole game.

What's your floor this week? Tell me below so it's on record. 💪
