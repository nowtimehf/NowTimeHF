Burnout rarely announces itself.

It doesn't usually look like collapse. It looks like this:

You're still hitting your deadlines. You're just not enjoying any of it.
You say yes automatically, then resent it quietly.
You're tired in a way sleep doesn't fix.
Things that used to feel manageable feel heavy for no obvious reason.

That's the stage where it's still easy to turn around. And it's exactly the stage most people talk themselves out of doing anything about, because nothing has technically gone wrong yet.

You don't have to be in crisis to deserve support. You just have to notice.

If you read that list and recognized yourself in three of them — that's the signal. Not a breakdown later.

Which one hit? No judgment here.
