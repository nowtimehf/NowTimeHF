Q4 is when burnout peaks and reporting goes silent.

The two happen together, and they're related.

Deadlines compress into a shorter month. Holiday cover means fewer people carrying the same load. Year-end reviews are close enough that nobody wants to be the person who says they're struggling. And there's an unspoken sense that everyone just has to get to January.

So the load goes up and the signal goes down. Your engagement survey in November will look better than October felt.

This is the specific reason I don't rely on self-reporting to know how a program is going. People under-report exactly when they're most stretched, which is exactly when you need to know.

Participation data doesn't have that problem. Someone quietly stopping is visible whether or not they're willing to say anything.

If you're planning a check-in this quarter, don't lead with "how's everyone doing?" You'll get "fine."

Look at who's stopped showing up to the things they used to. That's the honest answer.
