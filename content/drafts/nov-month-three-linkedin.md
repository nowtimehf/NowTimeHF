One question will tell you more about a wellness vendor than their entire sales deck.

"Can you show me a real engagement report from month three of an existing client?"

Not month one. Everything looks good in month one — novelty does the work.

Month three is where programs actually live or die. It's when the launch energy is gone, the reminders get ignored, and you find out whether anyone is structurally responsible for the people who drifted off.

Watch how the vendor answers.

If they send a case study with a testimonial and no numbers, that's an answer. If they talk about features instead of usage, that's an answer. If they say the data is confidential, ask for an anonymized version — any vendor who actually tracks this has one.

The good answer looks boring: a participation curve, a dip, and a specific description of what they did about the dip.
