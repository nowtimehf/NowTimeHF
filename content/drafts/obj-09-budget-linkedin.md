"Our budget is already committed this year."

Then don't buy a program. Buy one workshop.

I mean that literally. The worst version of this industry asks you to commit twelve months and a company-wide rollout to something you've never seen work on your actual team.

That's a bad deal, and it's why so many wellness contracts get signed once and never renewed.

So here's the alternative: one 60-minute live session, 30 days of platform access for whoever attends, and an engagement report at the end showing exactly who used what.

$1,500–$2,500 depending on team size. One-time. No multi-year anything.

If the data's good, you walk into next year's budget conversation holding evidence instead of a proposal. If it isn't, you're out one workshop fee and you learned something real about your team either way.

Small yes first. Earn the big yes.

I'd rather prove it on a fraction of your budget than argue for all of it.
