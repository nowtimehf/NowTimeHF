The most effective things I've ever coached anyone through cost nothing.

Walking after meals. Going to bed at the same time. Drinking water before coffee. Ten minutes of movement before you open your inbox. Eating an actual lunch.

No membership, no equipment, no app required.

I sell a program, so you'd expect me to argue otherwise. I won't. If money is the thing standing between you and starting, then start with the free version — it works, and it's most of the result.

What you're eventually paying for isn't the information. It's someone making sure you keep doing it after the motivation runs out.

But the doing? That part's free. Start there today.

Pick one from that list. Just one. Which is it?
