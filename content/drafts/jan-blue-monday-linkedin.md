Today is "Blue Monday," the most depressing day of the year.

It's made up. It started as a travel company's marketing campaign in 2005.

You'll see a lot of wellness posts today anyway. Here's a more useful number.

Burned-out employees are 63% more likely to take a sick day and 23% more likely to visit the ER. (Gallup)

That isn't one bad Monday. It shows up every week in absences, coverage gaps, and, eventually, your insurance renewal.

So skip the Blue Monday webinar.

Pull last quarter's unplanned absences by team. If one team is out of line with the rest, that's your burnout map. It took ten minutes and cost nothing.

Have you ever looked at absence data as a burnout signal?
