Your most burned-out employee probably hasn't told you.

Only 42% of burned-out employees tell their manager. And 42% of those who do say nothing changed. (Mind Share Partners)

So most of the burnout on your team is invisible to you by design.

It doesn't look like complaints. It looks like:

Cameras off more often.
No more volunteering for things.
Fast replies, short answers.
"I'm fine," with no follow-up question.

A survey won't catch it. They're the least likely to fill one in honestly.

What catches it is behavior: who showed up, who stopped, and whether anyone noticed.

What's the earliest sign of burnout you've seen in someone who never said a word?
