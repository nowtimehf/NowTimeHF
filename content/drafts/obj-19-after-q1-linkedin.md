"Let's revisit wellness after Q1."

I hear this every January. Fair. Q1 is busy.

But Q1 is when the resignations land.

The decisions were made in December. The letters arrive in January and February. By April, you're not revisiting wellness. You're backfilling.

One backfill costs 6–9 months of that person's salary. (SHRM)

Pick your most reliable mid-level person. Do that math. Then compare it to what it costs to find out, this month, who's running on empty.

Waiting until after Q1 isn't saving money. It's choosing which bill you pay.

What's the one role on your team you can't afford to backfill this spring?
