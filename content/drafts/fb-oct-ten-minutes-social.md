Build the plan around your worst day, not your best one.

Most people design for the version of themselves with a clear calendar, decent sleep, and some enthusiasm. That person shows up maybe twice a week.

The plan has to work for the other five days. The bad-sleep, back-to-back-meetings, nothing-left day.

If it survives that, it survives everything. If it only works when conditions are good, it isn't a plan — it's a mood.

Ten minutes on the worst day beats an hour you'll skip.

What does your worst-day version look like? Name it, so it exists before you need it.
