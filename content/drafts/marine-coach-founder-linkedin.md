I spent 15 years coaching individuals before I understood why companies fail at this.

Marine Corps first. Operation Iraqi Freedom. Then the gym floor — personal training, boxing, running gyms.

Fifteen years of watching one pattern repeat: a person changes when someone builds them structure and then actually checks in. Not when they're inspired. When they're accountable to something.

Then I started talking to HR leaders and found the corporate version of the same story. Good intentions. Real budgets. Programs with no structure and nobody following up.

Same failure, bigger scale.

That's why Now Time exists for companies and not just individuals. Not another app to download and forget — a system with the two things I've never once seen transformation happen without.

Structure. Accountability.

Everything else in this industry is a variation on those two, or a distraction from them.

We're onboarding corporate partners now, and watching gym-floor principles hold up at company scale has been the most interesting problem I've worked on.

More soon.
