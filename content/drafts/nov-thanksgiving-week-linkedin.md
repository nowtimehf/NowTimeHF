Thanksgiving week. Here's one thing worth doing before the long weekend, and it takes about four minutes.

Think of the two or three people on your team you never worry about. The ones who always deliver, never complain, and quietly absorb whatever lands on them.

Send each of them two lines. Specific, not generic.

Not "thanks for all you do." Something like: "The way you handled the Henderson handoff kept three other people from having a bad week. I noticed."

That's it. No meeting, no gift card, no survey.

The reliable ones are the least likely to be thanked specifically, because nothing they do ever becomes a visible problem. Their work is the absence of fires.

Recognition that names the actual thing is rare enough that people remember it for a long time. It costs nothing, and it's the cheapest retention move available to any manager.

Do it Wednesday, before everyone logs off.
