The last quarter of the year is heavier than people admit.

Deadlines compress. Colleagues take leave and their work doesn't. Holidays add a second set of obligations on top of the first. And there's a general agreement that everyone just needs to get to January.

Which is fine, except January is eleven weeks away, and "push through" is not a plan that lasts eleven weeks.

What actually works in Q4 is lowering the bar on purpose. Not stopping — shrinking. Fewer sessions, shorter ones, protected rather than ambitious.

You're not trying to make progress this quarter. You're trying to arrive in January without having lost the thread.

What are you shrinking rather than dropping between now and the new year?
