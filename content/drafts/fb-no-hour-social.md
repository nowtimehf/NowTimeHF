You don't need an hour.

That's the story that keeps most people stuck — that it doesn't count unless it's a full session, a real gym, a proper meal plan.

So the day gets away, the hour never appears, and you write off the whole thing until Monday.

Twenty minutes counts. Ten counts. A walk after lunch counts.

Not because it's optimal. Because it's the version that actually happens on a Tuesday when everything ran long.

The people I've coached who changed the most weren't the ones with the best schedules. They were the ones who stopped waiting for a good one.

What's the smallest version of your workout you'd actually do today? Tell me below.
