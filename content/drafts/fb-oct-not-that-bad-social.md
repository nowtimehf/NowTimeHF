"It's not that bad" is how most people describe something that has been slowly getting worse for a year.

The back that only hurts sometimes. The sleep that's fine, mostly. The stress that's just how it is right now — and has been right now since spring.

The trouble with gradual is that there's never a day when it becomes obviously a problem. Each week is roughly like the last, so the comparison you'd need to notice it never happens.

So make the comparison deliberately. Not against yesterday. Against a year ago.

Would last year's version of you say this is fine?

Answer that one honestly. It's usually the most useful question of the month.
