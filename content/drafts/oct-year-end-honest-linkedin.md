Two months left in the year. Here's what I'd want to know if I were you.

I'm a one-person company. I can take on a limited number of corporate partners at a time, and I'd rather say that plainly in October than find out in February that I've overcommitted and someone's program is suffering for it.

So if you're weighing this for next year, the useful version of the conversation is short: what's your headcount, what have you already got, and what would you need to see to justify continuing it.

Fifteen minutes. I'll tell you honestly whether the Pilot Sprint makes sense for your team or whether you'd be better served by something else — including nothing, which is sometimes the right answer for a company that's already doing this well.

I'm not going to build urgency that isn't real. There's no expiring discount and no fake scarcity. Just a calendar that fills.

If your people had a hard year and you'd like January to look different, this is a reasonable month to have the conversation.
