The people who look like they're handling everything are usually the ones nobody checks on.

It's a strange bind. Competence buys you a reputation for not needing anything, and then the reputation becomes the reason nobody asks.

So they keep absorbing. And because they're good at it, the absorbing is invisible until it isn't.

If someone in your life is the reliable one — the one who always has it handled — they are statistically the least likely person to be asked how they are.

Ask them anyway. Especially them.

Who's the person in your life that nobody checks on? Go check on them.
