Here's a number worth knowing about yourself: how many days in a row is your longest streak?

Not your best week. Not your peak. Your longest unbroken run of the thing you say matters.

Most people can't answer it, which is telling. We track weight, calories, PRs — all outcomes. Almost nobody tracks consistency, which is the only input that reliably produces those outcomes.

Streaks aren't about perfectionism. They're just honest. You can talk yourself into believing you've been consistent. A number won't let you.

Start counting. The first honest week is usually humbling and always useful.

What's your current streak on anything? Be honest — I'll go first: 9 days.
