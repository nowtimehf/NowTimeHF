Save this one for the first week of January. Three questions for your 2027 wellness plan.

1. What did we pay for in 2026, and how many people actually used it?

Not enrolled. Used. If you can't answer this, that's the answer. The industry average participation rate is 6–8%. If your vendor can't tell you whether you're above or below it, the first thing to fix is the reporting, not the program.

2. Who did we never reach?

Every program has a group that never shows up. Usually the busiest managers, the remote employees, and the reliable high performers who never complain. Name them specifically. That group is your 2027 target, not the people who were already doing fine.

3. Who owns the follow-up?

Most wellness programs launch well and then nobody is responsible for what happens in week three. If the honest answer is "the platform sends reminders," there's no owner. Pick a person, or pick a partner who will be that person.

You don't need a new strategy deck. You need clear answers to these three, written down, before the first budget conversation of the year.

Which of the three would be hardest for your team to answer today?
