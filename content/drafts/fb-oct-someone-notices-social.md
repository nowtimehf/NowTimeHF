The single biggest predictor of whether someone sticks with something isn't motivation, discipline, or how good the plan is.

It's whether anyone would notice if they stopped.

I've watched this hold for fifteen years across every kind of person. The ones who keep going almost always have someone — a coach, a friend, a group chat, a partner — for whom their disappearance would register.

That's not weakness. It's just how humans work. We're much better at keeping promises to other people than to ourselves.

If you keep starting and stopping, you probably don't need a better program. You need a witness.

Who's your witness right now? If the answer is nobody, that's the thing to fix first.
