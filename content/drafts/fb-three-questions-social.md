Three questions worth asking about your own routine this weekend:

1. What did I actually do this week — not what did I intend to do?
Be honest. Intentions don't produce results and they don't need protecting.

2. What's the one thing that keeps getting skipped?
That's usually the thing carrying the most weight. It's skipped because it's hard, not because it's optional.

3. Who would notice if I stopped?
If the answer is nobody, that's your real problem. Not discipline. Accountability.

Most people don't need a new plan. They need an honest look at the one they've got.

Answer #3 in your head. If it's nobody, reply here and I'll be that person for a while.
