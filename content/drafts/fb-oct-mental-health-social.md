World Mental Health Day is Saturday.

You'll see a lot of graphics. Most of them will be sincere and none of them will change your week.

So here's something you can actually use instead.

Pick one person this week and ask them how they're doing — then wait. Don't fill the pause. Most people give the polite answer first and the real one about four seconds later, if the silence holds.

That's it. That's the whole practice.

Awareness days are useful for starting conversations. They don't have the conversations for you.

Try the four seconds this week. It works better than you'd expect. 💬
