The easiest habit I know: a ten-minute walk after your biggest meal.

No gear. No gym. No change of clothes. Just shoes and the door.

It's short enough that you'll actually do it on a bad day, and it attaches to something you already do every day, which is the real secret to any habit that lasts.

It's also the best ten minutes of conversation you'll get all day. Bring someone.

Try it every day this week and tell me on Saturday how it went. 🚶
