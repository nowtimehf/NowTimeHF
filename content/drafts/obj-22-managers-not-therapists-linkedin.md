"My managers aren't therapists."

Correct. They shouldn't try to be.

They need three questions, not a counseling degree. Save these for your next 1:1:

1. "What's taking more energy than it should right now?"
2. "What's one thing I could take off your plate this week?"
3. "When did you last have a full day off with no messages?"

That's it. Ask them, listen, and act on one answer.

Then the manager's job is to point to what the company offers, and follow up next week.

Most managers want to help. They just don't know what to say or where to send people. Fix both and you've fixed most of it.

Which of the three would get the most honest answer on your team?
