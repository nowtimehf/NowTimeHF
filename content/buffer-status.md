**1. Buffer account timezone is `Asia/Makassar` (UTC+8).** The posts will *send*
at the right moment — the offsets are explicit — but Buffer's UI renders every
time in the account timezone, so a post set for 8:00 AM Eastern displays as
8:00 PM the same day. Change the account timezone to `America/New_York` before
reviewing, or every time will look twelve hours wrong.
