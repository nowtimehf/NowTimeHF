# Caption Bank

One short caption per card image, so **any card can be posted on its own** —
as an Instagram post, a repost, a comment reply, or a slot the full-length
post doesn't fill.

These are deliberately shorter than the paired posts in `content/drafts/`. Use
the draft when the card is illustrating a full argument; use the caption here
when the image *is* the post.

Alt text for every card lives in `cards.json`, `stats-cards.json`, and
`quotes.json` — copy it into the platform's alt field, it is not optional.

## System D — Stat cards (LinkedIn)

### `stat-01-seventy-six.png`

**On the card:** 76% of employees hit burnout at work at least sometimes. → *The average wellness program reaches 8% of them.* (Source: Gallup)

**Caption:**

The gap is the entire argument.

The 8% isn't random — voluntary, unstructured programs recruit the people who were already going to be fine. Great employees. They weren't the problem.

The 76% includes your quiet high performers. The ones nobody's worried about.

#CorporateWellness #HRLeadership #BurnoutPrevention

### `stat-02-six-to-nine.png`

**On the card:** 6–9 months of salary to replace one employee. → *A wellness program costs less than one departure.* (Source: SHRM)

**Caption:**

One director at $110k costs $55–82k to replace once you count recruitment, ramp, the productivity dip across their team, and what walks out the door with them.

Most companies I talk to lost three or four this year they'd privately call avoidable.

Preventing one covers the program. That's the whole business case.

#EmployeeRetention #HRLeadership #BudgetSeason

---

### `stat-03-forty-two.png`

**On the card:** 42% of burned-out employees tell their manager. → *You meet the other 58% at their exit interview.* (Source: Mind Share Partners)

**Caption:**

The exit interview is an excellent diagnostic and a terrible intervention. It only runs on people you've already lost.

Every HR leader I know has sat across from someone genuinely good and heard exactly what went wrong — all of it fixable eighteen months earlier.

The data you need isn't confidential. It's just not being collected.

#HRLeadership #EmployeeRetention #WorkplaceWellbeing

---

### `stat-04-eight-percent.png`

**On the card:** 6–8% average participation in corporate wellness programs. → *You are paying for 100%.* (Source: Industry benchmark)

**Caption:**

Run that against any other line item. Software licensed company-wide that 8% log into. Office space 8% use.

Any of those triggers a review. Wellness doesn't, because it's filed under "the right thing to do" rather than under things that are supposed to work.

What's your actual utilization? Not enrollment. Usage.

#CorporateWellness #BenefitsStrategy #HRLeadership
