Generate hook options for: `$ARGUMENTS`

Read `brand/brief.md` for voice rules and audience pain points — hooks must sound like the brand, not generic virality bait.

## Formulas to draw from (mix several, don't use one formula for every hook)
- **Contrarian**: state the belief everyone holds, then flatly reject it.
- **Specific number/result**: a concrete, surprising stat or outcome up front.
- **Curiosity gap**: promise a specific payoff without revealing it yet.
- **Direct pain callout**: name the audience's exact problem in their own words.
- **Mistake/warning**: "the [thing] that's costing you [specific cost]."
- **Before/after**: a sharp contrast between two states.
- **Question that presupposes a stance**: makes the reader argue with themselves.
- **Story cold-open**: drop into the middle of a specific moment, no setup.

## Rules
- Every hook must be concrete — real numbers, named specifics, or vivid detail. Reject anything that could apply to any topic ("You won't believe what happened...").
- No clickbait that the post can't pay off — the hook must be honest about what follows.
- Match the brand's voice rules from the brief exactly (formality, slang, emoji, sentence length).
- Generate the count requested after `--count`, default 10, deduped and covering at least 4 different formulas.

## Output
Numbered list, each hook on its own line, formula name in brackets after it. End with the 2-3 strongest picks and why, so the user isn't left to judge 10 options unaided.
